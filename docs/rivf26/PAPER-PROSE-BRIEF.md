## Decision 2: how much space the MarketMaker section gets

The supporting material, all in the paper kit:
- **The ladder figure:** naive rungs N0–N3 drawn against the faithful queue band, one panel for each window or view.
- **Main claim:** fill assumptions dominate. Own-price fills alone move krx2025 by about 72 pp over the whole window.
- **Naive falls outside the faithful band in both directions.** In im2022 every naive rung is below it. After the
  cutover, N0 (+36.70%) is far above it and N1–N3 are below it.
- **The queue band is a bound by construction:**
  - The optimistic arm assumes A is first in the queue; the conservative arm assumes A is behind everything displayed.
  - Caveat: the conservative arm can't place quotes beyond the 3 visible book levels (LIMITATIONS #3).
- **Markouts at 5 min, points per contract:** naive N3 is the most adversely selected on every span.
  - im2022: N3 −0.71, F-opt +0.41, F-con −0.57.
  - krx2025: N3 −2.22, F-opt −0.68, F-con −0.31.
- **No real fill data is available** (owner, R3.1). The defensible claim is: "the truth lies within F's band; naive
  can fall outside it in either direction".
- **A draft paragraph** is at the end of `REPORT.md`.

## Decision 3: the handoff's §6 acceptance criteria

The criteria as written expect "faithful fills fewer" (criterion 1) and "faithful loses more" (criterion 2). These
MarketMaker rows fail one or both (map, "Table II at a glance"):

| Row | Criterion 1 | Criterion 2 |
|---|---|---|
| im2022 F-opt (fills 153.5% of naive) | fail | fail |
| im2022 F-con | pass | fail |
| im2022 anchor | pass | fail |
| krx2025 pre-cutover F-opt (114.5%) | fail | pass |

These failures are consistent with the owner's framing: naive's bias has no fixed sign. So the criteria need
rewording, not the experiment tuning. Nothing was tuned.
